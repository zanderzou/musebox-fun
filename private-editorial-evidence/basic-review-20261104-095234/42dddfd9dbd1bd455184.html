Musebox AI — AI video and image generator

Musebox AI turns a still photo into a moving clip. Upload an image, pick a template, and the model animates it — no prompt engineering, no local GPU, no install. Generation runs on our own hardware and finishes in about a minute; results land in your collection, where you can download them or use them as the starting frame for the next clip.

Image-to-video is the core of it. Alongside it: text-to-image generation, extending a finished clip into a longer sequence, adding a soundtrack, and a character builder for keeping the same face across a whole set of shots. Output runs from 720p up to 1020p on the higher tiers, at five to twelve seconds a clip.

Templates cover most of what people ask for, but you can also train the platform on your own footage: upload a handful of clips and you get a private model that reproduces that subject in any template. Training runs unattended and takes a few hours.

Pricing is credits, not seats — you pay per generation, and a free balance comes with the account, so the first clips cost nothing. Payment is by card or crypto. The interface is available in 13 languages. Studios and developers can reach the same generation pipeline over a REST API with per-plan rate limits.

Common questions

Do I need a GPU or any software?

No. Everything runs in the browser against our own GPUs. There is nothing to install.

How long does one video take?

Around a minute for a standard clip. Longer clips and higher resolutions take proportionally more.

Is there a free tier?

Every account starts with a credit balance, so you can generate before paying anything. After that, credits are bought outright or come monthly with a plan.

Can I train a model on my own footage?

Yes. Upload your clips and the platform trains a private model from them, usable in any template afterwards.

Is there an API?

Yes — the same generation pipeline is available over REST, with rate limits set per plan.
